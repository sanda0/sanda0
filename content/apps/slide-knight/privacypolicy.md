_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Developer: **Made By Sandakelum**

Slide Knight ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android developed by Made By Sandakelum ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- The game has **no accounts** and asks for **no personal details** (name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location).
- Your progress is saved **only on your device**. We run no servers and do not receive your game data.
- The game shows **ads from Google AdMob**. Google may collect some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.
- **Purchases** (the optional "Remove Ads") are handled by **Google Play**. We never see your payment details.

## Information stored on your device

The game saves this on your phone, in the game's own private storage:

- levels completed, stars and best move counts
- your coin balance
- settings (sound, music, screen shake, vibration)
- whether you bought "Remove Ads"

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads (only when you choose to watch one, for example for a hint) and occasional ads between levels. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which is part of Google.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's **advertising ID** and other device identifiers
- your **IP address**, which can indicate your approximate location (country or city)
- **device and app information**, such as device model, operating system version and language
- **interactions with ads**, such as views and taps
- **diagnostic information**, such as crash and performance data of the ads SDK

This data is processed by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). See also [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Your choices**

- If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time with the **PRIVACY** button in the game's Settings.
- On any Android device you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (**Settings → Privacy → Ads**, or **Google → Ads**, depending on the device).
- Buying "Remove Ads" turns off the ads between levels. Rewarded ads stay available, and you only see them if you choose to.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy; we never receive them. The game only checks with Google Play whether you own "Remove Ads", so it can restore it after a reinstall or on a new phone.

## Permissions

The game, and the Google libraries it includes for ads and purchases, use these Android permissions:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet and network state | To load ads and talk to Google Play for purchases |
| Advertising ID | Used by Google AdMob as described above |
| Billing | To buy and restore "Remove Ads" through Google Play |
| Vibrate | Short vibrations during play (can be turned off in Settings) |
| Wake lock, foreground service, basic phone state | Used internally by Google's ads and billing libraries |

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

## Data deletion

We hold no data about you. To delete the game data on your device, uninstall the game or clear its storage in your phone's settings. For advertising data held by Google, use your device's ad settings or [Google's privacy controls](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
