_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Blocky Blasters ("the game") is made by The Pixel Forge ("we").

## Summary

The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

## What the game stores

The game saves the following **only on your device**:

- Your best score and best wave
- Achievement progress and game statistics (for example total kills)
- Your settings (controls layout, sensitivity, sound, graphics)

This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the game's data in your device settings.

## What the game does not do

- It does not use the internet. The app does not request internet access.
- It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal data.
- It does not contain ads, analytics or tracking tools.
- It does not offer in-app purchases.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example an online leaderboard), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the new data use will be described here.
