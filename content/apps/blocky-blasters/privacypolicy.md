_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Blocky Blasters ("the game") is made by The Pixel Forge ("we").

## Summary

We do not run any servers and do not collect your personal information ourselves. The game shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google collects some device data to show those ads, as described below.

## What the game stores on your device

The game saves the following **only on your device**:

- Your best scores, best wave and best night
- Achievement progress and game statistics (for example total kills)
- Your settings (controls layout, sensitivity, sound, graphics)

This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the game's data in your device settings.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ads:

- **Rewarded ads** that you choose to watch (for example to revive once, or to repair houses at dawn).
- **Interstitial ads** now and then between runs, never during play.

The ads come from Google AdMob, which needs internet access. When the game asks for an ad, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

- Your device's **advertising ID** and other device identifiers
- Your **IP address**, which can show your approximate location
- Device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language)
- How you interact with ads (views, taps)
- Crash logs and performance data of the ads SDK

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, limit how often they appear, and prevent fraud. Ad content is limited to a "Teen" rating at most.

How Google uses this data: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Your choices

- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required):** the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game under **Settings > Ad privacy > Privacy options**.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads on some devices).
- Without consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited data (for example for fraud prevention and frequency limits).

## What the game does not do

- It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.
- It has no accounts, chat or online multiplayer.
- It does not offer in-app purchases.
- We do not sell your data and do not use any analytics or tracking tools of our own.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example an online leaderboard), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
