Fast first-person survival shooter for Android. Hold a foggy graveyard against waves of blocky zombies, ghosts, robots and bosses, upgrade between waves and chase high scores. Plays offline, free with ads (never during play), no in-app purchases.

## Highlights

- Fixed 60 Hz deterministic simulation, separated from rendering, with seeded randomness
- Custom capsule-vs-AABB player collision instead of the engine's built-in movement
- Data-driven maps and tuning, validated by a map checker and a gdUnit4 test suite
- Touch controls with aim assist, auto-fire and a fully movable/resizable button layout
- 39 achievements, wave shop and combo scoring
